Front center edge. Must face forward. Tape flat with foam tape.

Upper middle. Close to motors so output wires stay short.

Dead center of chassis. Must be flat and level — it measures tilt!

Lower middle. Between battery and ESP32 power input.
5 — Battery pack
Bottom layer or rear of chassis. Lowest point = better balance.
* MLX90614 (future)

EVERY PIN — SENSOR TO ESP32
ToF
VL53L0X
Distance sensor — detects objects ahead
RedVCC→ ESP32 3.3V
BlackGND→ ESP32 GND
GreenSDA→ GPIO 21 (shared)
YellowSCL→ GPIO 22 (shared)
Tape to front edge of chassis with foam tape. Faces forward.
GY
MPU6050
Gyroscope + accelerometer — knows direction + tilt
RedVCC→ ESP32 3.3V (same as VL53)
BlackGND→ ESP32 GND (same as VL53)
GreenSDA→ GPIO 21 (same wire as VL53!)
YellowSCL→ GPIO 22 (same wire as VL53!)
AnyAD0→ GND (sets I2C address to 0x68)
SkipINT→ not needed for basic use
Mount flat and level at chassis center. Even a small tilt gives wrong angle readings.
M
TB6612FNG
Motor driver — controls both wheel motors
RedVCC→ ESP32 3.3V
BlackGND→ GND
BlackVM→ Battery+ (motor power, not 3.3V!)
PurpleSTBY→ GPIO 16
PurpleAIN1→ GPIO 17 (right motor dir)
PurpleAIN2→ GPIO 18 (right motor dir)
PurplePWMA→ GPIO 19 (right motor speed)
PurpleBIN1→ GPIO 25 (left motor dir)
PurpleBIN2→ GPIO 26 (left motor dir)
PurplePWMB→ GPIO 27 (left motor speed)
AOUT1/AOUT2 → right motor wires. BOUT1/BOUT2 → left motor wires.
5V
Buck converter (LM2596)
Steps battery 7.4V down to clean 5V for ESP32
RedIN+→ Battery positive
BlackIN−→ Battery negative
RedOUT+→ ESP32 5V pin (VIN)
BlackOUT−→ ESP32 GND
Turn the small screw on the buck converter until output reads exactly 5.0V with a multimeter before connecting to ESP32.
HOW ALL 3 SENSORS SHARE 2 WIRES
Each sensor has a unique address — like a phone number
VL53L0X 0x29 distance sensor
MPU6050 0x68 gyro (AD0 → GND)
MLX90614 0x5A thermal (future)
How to connect all 3 to the same 2 wires
On your breadboard, pick any empty column — call it the SDA column. Plug ALL sensor SDA pins into different rows of that same column. The breadboard connects them internally. Run one wire from that column to GPIO21. Done. Repeat for SCL on GPIO22. That's the entire I2C bus.
MPU6050 address conflict warning
MPU6050 has a pin called AD0. If AD0 is HIGH it uses address 0x69. If AD0 is LOW (connect to GND) it uses 0x68. Always connect AD0 to GND so the address is fixed and predictable.
ADDING MLX90614 THERMAL SENSOR LATER
IR
MLX90614
Detects body heat — confirms human vs wall
RedVCC→ 3.3V (same rail)
BlackGND→ GND (same rail)
GreenSDA→ GPIO 21 (same SDA column)
YellowSCL→ GPIO 22 (same SCL column)
Mount right next to VL53L0X on front edge. Both must point at the same target simultaneously for accurate detection.
Why MPU6050 makes your robot smarter
You already have MPU6050 — this replaces the need for wheel encoders for direction tracking! The gyroscope tells you exact heading angle in degrees. When the robot turns, MPU6050 measures exactly how many degrees it turned. Way more accurate than counting wheel pulses.
